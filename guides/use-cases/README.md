# TRACE Use-Case Guides

Worked guides for the situations in the README's [Apply TRACE](../../README.md#apply-trace) table. Each one narrows the framework to the dimensions that matter in that situation, gives the questions in the order you would actually ask them, and walks at least one hypothetical example end to end.

| Situation | Dimensions | Guide | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acting on an AI-generated alert (SOC, 5 minutes) | R, C, E | [soc-alert-triage.md](soc-alert-triage.md) | Published |
| Investigating an AI failure or adversary use of AI (IR) | A, E first; then T, R, C | — | Planned. The [`trace-investigate`](../../skills/trace-investigate.md) skill and the [case studies](../case-studies/) cover the ground until it exists. |
| Writing AI policy and vendor requirements (GRC) | All five | — | Planned. The [crosswalk](../../docs/TRACE-crosswalk.md) and the [`trace-vendor`](../../skills/trace-vendor.md) skill cover the ground until it exists. |

Examples in these guides are hypothetical unless they link to a [case study](../case-studies/). Vendor names are avoided; patterns are not.
